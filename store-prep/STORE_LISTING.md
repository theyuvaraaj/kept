## Full description (Play, max 4000 chars)

Kept is a habit tracker for habits that happen at a place — the gym, the track,
the library, the studio. Instead of tapping "done" on the couch, Kept checks
that you actually showed up.

HOW IT WORKS
• Pick a habit and pin its spot (use your current location).
• Choose your schedule — specific weekdays, or "any N days a week".
• When you're there, check in. Kept confirms you're within range.
• Turn on Auto check-in and Kept marks the day for you when you arrive — no
  tapping needed.

STAY HONEST, STAY CONSISTENT
• 🔥 Streaks that only count the days you really showed up.
• A forgiving grace day so one slip doesn't wipe your progress.
• "Kept %" — your real attendance, per habit and overall.
• Weekly and monthly summaries.
• A GitHub-style yearly heatmap of every day you kept.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account. No sign-up.
• Your habits and history stay on your device — nothing is uploaded, sold, or
  tracked. No ads. No analytics.
• Location is used only on your device to confirm you're at your spot.

Kept is simple on purpose: choose your spots, show up, keep the chain alive.
